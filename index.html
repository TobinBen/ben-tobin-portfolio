About me.

I'm Ben Tobin, a journalist focused on business, economic development, real estate and the ways major decisions affect local communities. My reporting has appeared in Triangle Business Journal, Business Insider and the Louisville Courier Journal.

My work combines original sourcing, public records and data to make complex stories accessible and hold decision-makers accountable.

I'm also pursuing a master's degree in analytics at the Georgia Institute of Technology, sharpening the quantitative skills I bring to my reporting.

At the Courier Journal, I contributed to the newsroom's coverage of former Kentucky Gov. Matt Bevin's controversial pardons, part of the work recognized with the 2020 Pulitzer Prize for Breaking News Reporting.
